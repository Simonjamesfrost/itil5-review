# Major Incident Procedure – Lab Systems

**Owner:** Service Integration Lead (internal)
**Version:** 0.2 draft

## Purpose

How we handle major incidents affecting lab systems, which are supported by three suppliers:

- **Hosting:** CloudCo (infrastructure and backups)
- **Application support:** AppWorks (Lab Results Portal and LIMS)
- **Network and service desk:** NetServe

The internal Service Integration team coordinates the suppliers.

## What counts as a major incident

- Priority 1 in the supplier's own ticketing tool.

## Steps

1. The service desk (NetServe) logs the incident in its tool and assigns it to the supplier it thinks is responsible.
2. That supplier investigates. If it believes the cause is with another supplier, it reassigns the ticket.
3. Each supplier works to its own contract resolution target (CloudCo 4 hours, AppWorks 6 hours, NetServe 2 hours).
4. The Service Integration Lead is told by email if the incident is still open after 4 hours.
5. Once fixed, the supplier that resolved it closes the ticket.

## Communication

The service desk emails users when the service is back.

## Reporting

Each supplier includes major incidents in its monthly service report to the Service Integration team.

## Review

A post-incident review is held if the business asks for one.
