# Organisation context

## Who the reports are for

- Product owners, service owners, delivery leads and PMO reviewers.
- Readers want a quick verdict, the main gaps and practical fixes. They do not want ITIL theory.

## Document types we usually review

- Product briefs and product roadmaps
- Service design packages
- Release, deployment and transition plans
- Operational runbooks and support models
- Service level documents and service reviews
- Supplier or acquisition proposals
- Improvement proposals

## House rules

- UK English.
- Plain language. Avoid jargon unless the document itself uses it.
- Regulated environment: where a document touches validated systems, data integrity or audit trail, flag gaps in change control, testing evidence and approvals as high priority.
